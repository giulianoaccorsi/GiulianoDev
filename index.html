About me

Hi, I’m Giuliano — but most people know me as Giu.
I’m an iOS Engineer who believes great apps are built where clean code meets delightful design. Over 6 years of experience, I’ve crafted apps from fintech to creative tools, always chasing that balance of performance and simplicity.

Outside of code, you’ll catch me playing video games, board games, sketching new app ideas, or tinkering with languages I probably should’ve mastered already.

This space is where I share projects, ideas, and experiments — fueled by coffee, curiosity, and the drive to keep learning.

Latest projects

PokeStudy

The PokeStudy app uses the PokeAPI to display detailed information about Pokémon, including stats, abilities, and types. Tapping on a Pokémon will route you to the details screen, which contains comprehensive information about the Pokémon, such as attributes, abilities, and weaknesses.

Technologies: SwiftUI, MVVM, Combine, Unit Test
